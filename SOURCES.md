## Russia (RU) — added 2026-10-02 by the RU-sources agent

Sampling (since 2026-10-02, topic-neutral): no keyword filter is used for collection. For the last 90 days, ria_ru / tass_com / rt_com / rt_ru keep their dense window (RIA: broad politics/security URL-slug list RECENT_SLUG_RU; tass.com: politics/world/russia/defense; rt.com: /news/ + /russia/; russian.rt.com: /russia/ + /world/), stored as `sample: recent`.
