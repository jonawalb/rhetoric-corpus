## Russia (RU) — added 2026-10-02 by the RU-sources agent

Sampling (since 2026-10-02, topic-neutral): no keyword filter is used for collection. For the last 90 days, ria_ru / tass_com / rt_com / rt_ru keep their dense window (RIA: broad politics/security URL-slug list RECENT_SLUG_RU; tass.com: politics/world/russia/defense; rt.com: /news/ + /russia/; russian.rt.com: /russia/ + /world/), stored as `sample: recent`. All dates 2021-01-01 → now are covered by a section-based sample defined in `collectors/ru_statemedia.py` (`Site.sample_sections`, `select()`): whole sections are taken in full (`sample: section`); capped groups take a uniform random subset (`section_random`, or `random` for RIA) = the `cap` URLs with the smallest sha256(`"rhetoric-corpus-ru-statemedia-2026-10-02|" + url`), fetched round-robin by month. Rows carry `section` (from the URL) or, for RIA, `tags` (the page's own rubric/tag list). mil_ru stores every item (`sample: all`). Rows collected earlier under the old nuclear/strategic prefilter (`collectors/ru_common.py` KEYWORD_RE + nuclear URL-slug lists) keep `sample: backfill` (rt_com 2,965, rt_ru 2,611) or, for mil_ru, no `sample` field (95 rows) — they are topic-biased toward nuclear/strategic content; exclude them when computing topic shares. KEYWORD_RE is kept for term reports. Other `sample` values: rss (every RSS item) and seed (an article URL found by web search for one colleague's plutonium question, fetched normally; `collectors/ru_seed.py`). Article HTML is no longer cached in raw/ (only RIA monthly sitemaps); existing raw/ article caches (~3.8 GB) are kept as a free re-parse source.
